## Do I need a commercial license?

**No, if you're using Open Loop as a tool.** Installing the extension and server to report bugs in your own apps, at home or at work, doesn't require one. Your own apps aren't affected by Open Loop's license.

**No, if you share your changes.** You can modify Open Loop and distribute it, or run it as a network service, as long as you release your complete source code under the AGPL-3.0 to everyone you give it to or who uses it over a network.

**Yes, if you want to keep your changes closed.** For example, if you want to:

- ship Open Loop, or a product built from its code, without releasing your source under the AGPL;
- build Open Loop into a proprietary product, extension or service;
- offer a hosted or managed version without publishing your modifications;
- sell or relicense Open Loop's code under other terms.

A commercial license removes the AGPL's obligations for your use, and it can include support and custom terms.
